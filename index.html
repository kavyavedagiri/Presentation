06

Making AI a predictable workflow step

Configurable AI agents inside Camunda workflows

The problem

Traditional workflows are deterministic, but some steps require AI-driven judgement based on user input, business context, or external knowledge. Hard-coded rules are difficult to maintain as requirements evolve, while unrestricted AI responses can produce inconsistent outputs that downstream workflow steps cannot reliably consume. The challenge was to integrate configurable AI capabilities into an existing workflow platform while keeping outputs structured, predictable, and usable by subsequent steps.

What I built

⚙

Agent settings

Developer-configured prompts and knowledge sources.

The workflow remains predictable because the AI step returns a defined output that downstream forms can consume.

Knowledge retrieval (RAG)

Documents are chunked, embedded, and stored in Pinecone. At runtime, relevant chunks are retrieved and supplied to the language model alongside the prompt.

My contribution: agent settings and workflow integration. The backend team built the RAG pipeline.
